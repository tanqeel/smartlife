Voice alarms — screen off, phone in pocket

Phone on, screen off, in your pocket. No internet needed.

1
Turn voice on & allow notifications
One tap unlocks sound, voice and notifications.
2
Keep SmartLife open in the background
Open it, lock the screen, do not swipe it away. It rings and speaks at each block until you tap Dismiss or Snooze. Android can pause background pages — Keep-awake or the Clock app is the guaranteed path.
3
Keep-awake mode for Fajr & study blocks
Turn on “Keep screen awake” below and SmartLife holds the screen on (Wake Lock) — use it plugged in overnight for Fajr, or during a 40-minute study block. This is the strongest mode a web app can offer.
4
If you close the app completely — use your Clock app too
Honest limit: no web page can ring after you swipe it closed — the API built for that (Notification Triggers) never launched, and a closed page has no voice. So for full safety, copy today’s alarm times into your phone’s built-in Clock app (one tap below, or download the calendar file). The Clock app rings even with the app closed; SmartLife gives it the exact times. A native Android version (Capacitor) is the planned step that removes this limit entirely — same app, plus true closed-app alarms.
